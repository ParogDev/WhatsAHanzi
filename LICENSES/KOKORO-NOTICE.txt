Syllable recordings in Sounds/

The .ogg files in Sounds/ (one per Mandarin syllable and tone, named in numbered pinyin:
ni3.ogg, lv4.ogg for lü4, ma5.ogg for the neutral tone; probe.ogg is a copy of ni3.ogg) were
synthesised once with the Kokoro-82M v1.1-zh text-to-speech model, voice zf_001, by the
WhatsAHanzi companion's gen_audio.py. No human voice was recorded.
