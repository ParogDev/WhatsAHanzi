Game names in Data/Game.lua

The Chinese and English names of zones, dungeons, bosses, classes, races, factions,
professions, items and spells in Data/Game.lua are the names World of Warcraft itself
shows its players. They belong to Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. and are reproduced here
only so that this addon can recognise them in chat and show the matching name in the
other language, the same way localisation libraries such as LibBabble have done for
years. No ownership of these names is claimed, and this file does not place them under
the addon's own licence.

The names were taken from the client's data tables as exported by https://wago.tools
(build recorded at the top of Data/Game.lua). The player slang in the same table comes
from companion/data/slang.yaml in the WowForeverScaffolding repository and is released
under the addon's licence.
